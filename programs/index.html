01 3-Week Program

Foundation

Three Week Basic Obedience.

A foundation program designed to improve communication, manners and everyday control. Final content and program length may be adjusted after evaluation.

  • Sit, down, come, stay and place
  • Leash manners and structured walking
  • Doorway and vehicle manners
  • Household boundaries and no jumping
  • Impulse control and engagement
  • Confidence-building exercises
  • Owner handoff and instruction
02 6-Week Program

Development

Six Week Advanced Obedience.

A more complete program for stronger reliability, advanced control and off-leash goals. Each dog must be evaluated to determine suitability, expected results and the appropriate length of training.

  • Everything included in the basic foundation
  • Individualized off-leash development
  • Greater distance and longer duration
  • Reliable response around distractions
  • Public and real-world training
  • Advanced heel, recall, stay and place
  • Socialization and confidence building
  • Detailed owner transition session
